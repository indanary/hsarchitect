ZG House

ZG House is a residential project in Semarang located on a corner (hook) site. The design features a slanted mass composition with a layered zigzag façade, allowing maximum airflow, effective cross ventilation, and optimal natural lighting by orienting the house toward the angled side.

This slanted form enables air circulation from three sides, enhancing indoor comfort throughout the house. The origami-like zigzag geometry gives ZG House a strong iconic identity, expressing a dynamic and contemporary architectural character.
